# Outreach email drafts

Five drafts, one per lead. Each references something specific and verifiable from the
business's own Google listing — that specificity is the whole point, so don't flatten
these into one template.

## Before you send

1. **Confirm the email address.** No public email address was found for any of these
   five, which is normal for businesses without a website. Ring the number on the lead
   sheet and ask who to send it to, or message the Facebook/Instagram page. Do not guess
   an address, and don't send to a scraped personal Gmail found in a directory.
2. **Re-check the Google listing.** Confirm the business still has no website, and that
   the rating and review count you quote are current. Details were captured 2026-07-25.
3. **Paste the demo link.** Replace `{{DEMO_LINK}}` with that business's hosted concept
   page. See `../README.md` for hosting.
4. Send from a real signed address with a phone number. These are cold approaches to
   small owner-run businesses — one follow-up at most, and stop if they say no.

Gmail drafts of all five were created in the account with the recipient left blank.
